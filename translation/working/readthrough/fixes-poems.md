## Summary

Verse and classical Chinese passages found in the body (Introduction to the end of the Conclusion): 61 quotations.

Already carrying Kuki's original in the readable (no entry): 21. These are the thirteen *Book of Changes* quotations in Chapter 2 §16 that already stand in full in the body (兩儀生四象 to 群疑亡也); the *Shiji* line 武王俯取以祭 (p. 159); the two *Changes* lines in Chapter 3 §5 (p. 214); the *Kyōgyōshinshō* line in c3s9-7; the Xiang commentary line in c3s11-16; Valéry's *sourires jumeaux* (body); the *Nirvana Sutra* line in c3s14-15; the *Jōdoron* gāthā in conc2-12.

Fixed: 40 quotations in 37 new footnotes (the six pillow-word phrases of p. 63 are grouped in pairs as Kuki groups them), 55 entries in all. By section: Liezi (c1s4); Nāgasena Sūtra x2 (c1s6); Man'yōshū XVI x2, Prince Toneri's order, the Kakyō hyōshiki "rikai" poem (c2s2); pillow words x6, the Liu Xiyi couplet, the Kojiki song, Narihira's acrostic, the New Year palindrome (c2s3); Book of Documents (c2s9); Lucretius (c2s12); Changes x5 where the readable had cut the Chinese down to a fragment (c2s16); Xici zhuan 六爻相雜唯其時物也 (c2s17); Nāgasena x2, Madhyama Āgama, Tattvasiddhi (c2s21); Man'yōshū IV, V, III (c3s1); the tsujiura verse (c3s10); Faust 1759 and 1700 in Kuki's Japanese, Henjō (c3s11); Kyōgyōshinshō preface, Madhyama Āgama x2 (c3s12); Shuogua zhuan (conc2). Source: the literal for c2s2 (poems), c2s3 and c2s16, each checked against the image; the page images for all the rest. Where the Chinese stood inline in the body in cut-down form (c2s16, c2s17) or in full (the c2s3 couplet), it moves to the footnote. Four "(tidy)" entries trim the translations from c2s3-10 to c2s3-13, which would otherwise repeat the new footnotes; they can be skipped without breaking anything else.

Not fully established: in the Chinese passages read from the images (Liezi, Book of Documents, Nāgasena, Shuogua) Kuki's kunten are omitted and the placing of 、 is read from small print and should be checked once more; in c3s1-v1 the span of the ruby まね (on 數多) needs a closer look; the translations of the pillow-word phrases (c2s3-v1 to v3) are new, since the literal gives only romanisation; and those of c2s3-v4 to v7 are taken from the readable's own notes for the same reason.

Excluded as prose: the Kojiki narrative passages (pp. 149-157), Ecclesiastes, Sōseki, Hōnen's *Wago tōroku* and *Shūi Wago tōroku*, the *Genji*, *Hōjōki* and *Heike* phrases, Lear and Bottom, Novalis, Valéry's *Variété*, Watsuji on *renku*, and the 428571 number pun.

Literal note: p. 156, the literal prints 六爻相雜、唯其時物也; the image has no 、 and marks 時 with emphasis.

## Literal problems

- p. 184 (コマ 99, right page, line 1): the literal opens "That Bhikkhu Nāgasena asked Milinda in return, 'Why are these trees not the same?' Milinda answered ...", which reads as a subordinate clause with no main verb. The image has かの那先比丘は彌蘭に反問して『此等樹木何故不同』と云った。, a complete sentence in which かの is "the aforementioned" (Nāgasena of Chapter 1, section 6). Suggest: "The bhikkhu Nāgasena, whom we have met, asked Milinda in return, 'Why are these trees not the same?'" The readable's "Nāgasena, whom we met earlier," is a fair rendering of かの and needs no fix.
- p. 181 (コマ 97, left page): Kuki's reference "Met. Δ. 30, 1024b" is as printed and both versions follow it; Metaphysics Δ 30 runs at 1025a. A source slip, not a literal error; a Tr. note may be wanted.

## Summary

Counts: ADDS 3, DROPS 3, CHANGES 1, RULE 0 (7 fixes).

Not entered as fixes: across section 16 and the start of section 17 the readable consistently shortens Kuki's parenthetical Chinese to a key phrase (會通 for 聖人有以見天下之動、而觀其會通; 易與天地準 for the full 易與天地準、故能彌綸天地之道; 天地相遇; 天地絪縕…男女構精; 往遇雨則吉 for the whole Kui line statement; 唯其時物也 for 六爻相雜、唯其時物也), and drops the gloss (噬嗑す). This looks like a deliberate convention of the readable version; if the convention is that Kuki's Chinese is kept in full, these six places need restoring from the literal.

The range is close to the literal: the faults are small glosses and Western dates placed in the body without 〔 〕, and three dropped phrases, none of which changes Kuki's argument. Emphasis (all *time*, *by chance*, *instant*, *together*, *connection*, *is* / *necessarily is* italics) is preserved, and there are no dashes or banned words in the range.

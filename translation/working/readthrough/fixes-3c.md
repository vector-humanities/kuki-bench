## Literal problems

- p. 313: literal "must be something that suggests the correlation of being and nothingness". The OCR of コマ 163 reads 有と無との相關を明示してゐるものでなければならない; 明示 is "clearly shows" or "makes plain", stronger than "suggests".
- p. 312: literal "it should be thought that ... one has unawares strayed" drops the であらう of 考へるべきであらう; "it should probably be thought" is closer.
- p. 319 and p. 325: literal "we have attempted" and "We said ... We also said" supply a subject. The OCR shows none (偶然性の闡明を計つた; と云つた ... ことも云つた), so the readable "I" is not a breach of the 我々 = "we" convention and should not be corrected to "we" on that ground.

## Summary

Counts: ADDS 7, DROPS 0, CHANGES 12, RULE 0 (19 entries). Citations, page markers (pp. 285-331) and Kuki's italics are complete; no dashes or banned words.

Patterns not entered line by line: (a) the readable drops Kuki's quotation marks around "the necessary-contingent" after its first use in each paragraph, which loses his marking of the coined term; (b) the readable renders 無の可能 and 無いことの可能 as "the possibility of nothingness / of not being" where the literal has "the possible", and note c3s14-6 quotes "the possible of nothingness" while the text above it says "possibility", so text and note should be brought into line once the term is decided.

The range is close to the literal in substance, and its faults are small: inferential connectives Kuki does not use ("therefore", "consequently", "so that", "This is why"), two hedges converted to "I think" against the 思ふ convention, and Kuki's hyphenated compounds unpacked into "and". The most serious items are the Groundwork title substituted for Kuki's own (p. 294), "I think" for であらう (pp. 312, 330), the "necessity-contingency" complex rendered against note c3s12-1 (p. 291), and "the necessity of not being" against the glossary (p. 322).

## Literal problems

- p. 266 (right page of コマ 140): the image reads 「傍に在ること」(Sein-bei)として現前の「頽落」を意味してゐる. The literal (and the readable after it) gives "the 'falling' of the present", but the word is 現前 (presence, making-present, Heidegger's *Gegenwärtigen*), not 現在 (the present), which Kuki uses in the same sentence for "encounter in the present". Suggest "the 'falling' of presence" in both versions, with a footnote.
- p. 271 (left page of コマ 142): the image has 基礎的存在論的情態性, "fundamental-ontological state-of-mind" (from Heidegger's *Fundamentalontologie*). Both versions give "basic ontological state-of-mind", which loses the reference to fundamental ontology. Since the house rules allow "fundamental" only in titles, this needs a decision; at minimum footnote c3s10-2 could name 基礎的存在論.
- p. 277: the literal places its 〔Tr.〕 note on 不思議 after "if one stresses truth", well after the first occurrence of "the marvellous" ("Truth without the marvellous"). The readable puts the note at the first occurrence. Move the literal's note.
- p. 266: "(see p. 283)" is confirmed by the image (二八三頁參照). p. 283 of this printing is in section 11 (ceramics, *renku*) and has nothing on the "oldest" contingency; the readable's footnote c3s9-9 already says so.
- p. 254: the image confirms 微視的偶然は巨視的地平に於てのみ成立するものとして, so the literal's "taking microscopic chance as something that holds only on the macroscopic horizon" is correct, odd as it reads.

## Summary

ADDS 4; DROPS 4; CHANGES 6; RULE 0 (no dashes or banned words in the range; no aphoristic closers or "not X but Y" framing found). The readable version of sections 8 to 11 follows the literal closely, and its footnotes are accurate; the faults are small connectives, dropped qualifiers ("in general", "in truth", "himself") and two modal shifts (Kant's "ought to belong", Kuki's "may be thought" turned into "Since"), none of which reverses Kuki's argument.

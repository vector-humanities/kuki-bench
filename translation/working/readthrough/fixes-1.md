## Literal problems

- p. 3 (コマ 8, left page): the literal has "the quantitative relation between all the possible cases and the contingent cases in which an event occurs or does not occur". The Japanese (一事象の生起および不生起の總ての可能的な場合と、その事象の生起する偶然的な場合) attaches "occurrence or non-occurrence" to the possible cases, and the contingent cases are those in which the event occurs. The readable has this right.
- p. 19 (コマ 16, left page): literal "existing together with another thing, even by chance". The image has 偶然にも他のものと合つて, "by chance"; "even" over-reads にも. The readable's "by chance" is right.
- p. 21 and p. 26 (コマ 17 left, コマ 20 right): literal "so long as" for 以上は (重さが...偶然的徴表に過ぎない以上は; 絶對的同一關係...がない以上は). 以上は means "since, given that"; the readable's "since" is right. The literal's other "so long as" renderings (for 限り) are correct.
- p. 37 (コマ 25, left page): literal "an interval in time comes to mean what does not exist except after leaving an interval". The image has また間隔はやがて間隔を置いてより存在せぬものを意味する; やがて is "in turn, eventually", not "in time". The readable's "in turn" is right.

## Summary

ADDS 15, DROPS 5, CHANGES 21, RULE 0. Total 41.

No em or en dashes or banned words in the range. The serious faults are a handful of factual or attributional changes: "America and our country" added to the post-war case (p. 35), "possible content" and Kuki's "not fixed and static" dropped (pp. 14, 41), Lotze's view made to sound like Kuki's (p. 22), the dream claim softened (p. 38), and emphasis put in the wrong sentence (p. 33). The rest are smaller: dropped hedges ("I think", "almost", "probably", "may"), glosses inserted outside 〔 〕 in the *ma* etymology (p. 37), citations moved out of Kuki's form (Shōwa dates, "Forschung"), and a few glossary slips ("surface of contact", "predominant feature", 學問 as "sciences").

The readable follows Kuki closely through both sections and adds little interpretation of its own. Besides the entries above, it normalises Kuki's citations throughout ("Aristoteles, Metaphysica" to "Aristotle, *Metaphysics*", "Ib." to "ibid.", "3 Aufl." to "3. Aufl.", "9 éd." to "9e éd."), which goes against the convention that citations are kept in his form. This needs a global decision, so it is not entered line by line.

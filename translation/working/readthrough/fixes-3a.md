The readable version of pp. 185-242 follows the literal closely: the logic of sections 2, 3, 6 and 7 (the modal tables, Lewis, Becker, Hartmann, the three systems and their figures) is rendered without additions, the footnotes check out on dates and cross-references (Ch. 1 s. 5 for *tamatama* and *wakuraba ni*, Ch. 2 s. 5 for )( and Naruse), and there are no dashes or banned words.

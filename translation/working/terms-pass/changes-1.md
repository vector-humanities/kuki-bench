## Notes

- readable pp. 31, 32, 33: "the individual thing or the individual being" (three times) translates 個物または個體 / 個物、個體. 個體 is not 存在 or 有, so left unchanged under the rule. Since "being" is now reserved for 有, a reader may take these as 有. The literal file has "the individual"; aligning the readable to "the individual thing or the individual" (or "individual entity") would remove the clash. Coordinator's call.
- readable p. 32 / literal p. 31: Husserl's "the mere factual existence of a valid rule" translates 事實的存立 (Husserl's *Bestand*), not 存在. Left unchanged; "factual subsistence" would keep "existence" for 存在 only.
- literal p. 19: "existing *together* (σύν) with another thing" translates 存立して (存立, subsist). Left unchanged; the readable has "coming to stand ... together", which avoids the clash. Possible literal fix: "subsisting *together*".
- literal pp. 18, 21, 24, 25, 43: "exists" / "existing" translate 存する (偶然性が存する, 必然的關係の存する, 間に存する同一性, 內部に存する). Not 存在; left unchanged.
- readable p. 15 / literal p. 15: "mode of existence" = 存在樣態, already correct. "confers reality" = 實在性, not 存在.
- p. 16 "living being" = 生物; p. 39 "human beings" = 人間; p. 37 "exists" = 存在せぬ (already correct); pp. 39-41 "existence"/"exist" = 存在 throughout (already correct). Unchanged.
- No 無 used as a term in this chapter: p. 30 "nothing they did not do" = 無所不爲 (classical Chinese), p. 32 Husserl "there is no contingency" = 偶然は無い (ordinary negation).
- Outside my range: the literal file's glossary (lines 28-31) still lists 存在 = being, 有 / 無 = being / nothing, 非存在 = non-being, 極限的存在 = limit-being. It will need updating to match the new rule.

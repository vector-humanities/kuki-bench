## Notes

All page images for pp. 47-115 were checked against the entries above. Doubtful cases left unchanged:

- p. 53 (both files): "absolute being" translates 絶對有 and is already correct under the rule.
- p. 53 (readable "finds a place to exist"; literal "finds a place of existence"): 存在の場所. Both conform; no change.
- pp. 48, 59-63, 65, 67, 110, 111 ("exists", "existing", "exist"): these render 存する / 存しない / 存在する in ordinary predicate use and already read as "exist(s)". No change.
- p. 65 and p. 75 (both files): "ground of being" (twice on p. 65, once on p. 75) translates 實在理由 (ratio essendi), not 存在 or 有. Left unchanged, but "being" is now reserved for 有, so "real ground" or "ground of reality" would avoid a false signal. Your call.
- p. 49 (both files): the Schopenhauer "sufficient reason of being" was changed to "existence" because Kuki writes 存在の充足理由の原理; the Latin *essendi* stays as printed.
- p. 86 (both files): "maintaining its own existence" translates 自己の生存を保つ (生存, survival or life), not 存在. Left unchanged; "maintaining its own life" would be closer.
- p. 94 (both files): "the being 'without purpose'" translates 「目的なき」こと, not 有 or 存在. Left unchanged; "the 'without purpose'" or "the fact of being 'without purpose'" would keep "being" free for 有.
- p. 107 (readable): "a being able to act by 'design'" translates 意圖によつて行爲し得るもの, and "beings able to design purposive action" translates 意圖し得る者; p. 108 (readable) "a being able to act by design" likewise translates もの. Not 存在. Left unchanged; "something able to act" / "those able to design" (as the literal file has) would avoid the clash.
- p. 109, p. 114: "human being(s)" translates 人間. No change.
- p. 87, p. 88: 「自然の無力」 / 「無力」 (Ohnmacht) is "impotence", not 無. No change. p. 83, p. 114: 無目的 stays "purposelessness".

## Notes

- Checked and already correct under the rule (both files): p. 115 非存在 "non-existence"; p. 126 存在するか否か "exists", その存在を肯定 "its existence"; p. 127 神々の存在 "existence of the gods"; p. 129 干與の存在する宇宙 "interference exists"; p. 130 存在しなかつた "existed"; p. 134 存否 "exists" (readable) / "existence or non-existence" (literal), 因果性以外の關係の存在 "existence of a relation"; p. 136 同時に存在し "exist simultaneously"; p. 146 偶然の存在 "existence"; p. 163 (Proust) 同時に存在し "exist simultaneously"; p. 175 能力が存在する "exist"; p. 180 存在せしめた "brought into existence".
- p. 157, both files: Mill's phrase 偶然によつて同時に存在しまたは一が他に繼起する is rendered "coexist, or one follows the other, by chance". Left unchanged because it echoes Mill's own English ("co-exist"), but p. 136 renders the same Japanese "exist simultaneously". If consistency is preferred: "exist simultaneously, or one follows the other, by chance".
- p. 143, both files: "cannot in reality exist" / "cannot exist" renders あり得ない, not 存在; left.
- 存する (not 存在) is rendered "exists" / "existing" / "lies in" in several places (p. 135 literal "existing among many events", p. 138 "what causal relation, then, exists", p. 144 literal "there exists the positive contingency", p. 159, p. 168, p. 170). Outside the rule; left.
- p. 128: 「ここにもまた神々がある」 "Here too there are gods" renders ある, not 存在; left.
- p. 183 (Schelling): 在る / 必然的に在る renders "is"; outside the rule; left.
- 無 in this range occurs only in compounds of ordinary negation (無目的 purposelessness, 無意識的, 無內容, 無生物, 無關心 indifference, 無關係, 無智/無知 ignorance, 無數, 無限/無限定/無際限/無窮, 無造作, 無解決); none is 無 as nothingness, so no changes. 有 occurs only as 有つ/有する (possess) or in classical Chinese quotations (頤中有物, 聖人有以見, 當知此業有); none is 有 as being.
- The "human being(s)" and gerund uses of "being", and "nothing other than" etc., were skipped as non-terms.
